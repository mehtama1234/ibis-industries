Company Clusters

These are the dominant business-model groupings implied by the company universe. They are not stock screens. They are operating clusters: what sort of business this is, what force set governs it, and which owner type tends to win.

How to use it

Use this page to move from named companies to recurring business-model logic. The point is to see which clusters are structurally advantaged, which force signals repeat inside them, and what investors or operators should actually underwrite before backing a name in the cluster.
