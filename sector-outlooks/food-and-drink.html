Food & Drink

Food & Drink in 2025-2026 is being reorganized by Wellness Recodes Daily Life, Barbelled Consumer America, Scale, Financialization, and the Owned Economy, with the economics increasingly determined by who can absorb complexity and sit on the right side of sector bottlenecks.

This sector now has to answer to health behavior, ingredient volatility, and channel power at the same time.Prefer health-aligned, functional, premium, or distribution-advantaged positions over generic volume-dependent indulgence stories.
Where it shows up

Representative industries

Food & Drink

Airline Catering Services

Airline catering is a low-margin, high-volatility business where catering companies scramble to manage fuel price shocks and tariffs while airlines cut back economy meal service and shift growth to premium cabins, all against a backdrop of seven-year-long labor disputes and emerging AI waste-reduction technologies.

Food & Drink

Bagel Stores

The U.S. Bagel Stores industry is estimated at about $1.45 billion in 2025 revenue, up modestly from $1.389 billion in 2022 as higher menu prices, return-to-office traffic and chain expansion offset weak margins, high labor costs and competition from coffee shops, bakery cafes and fast-food breakfast menus.

Food & Drink

Bakery Cafes

The US bakery cafe industry is a mature but still growing fast-casual segment, with 2025 revenue of $18.6 billion and 1.6% growth as menu inflation, breakfast demand, beverage attach rates and franchise expansion offset traffic sensitivity and labor pressure.

Food & Drink

Breweries in the US

The US beer industry is contracting sharply—production down 5.7% in 2025, craft breweries down to 9,344 in June 2026, and 481 closures in 2025—while large producers consolidate, shift to premium and non-alcoholic products, and face tariff-driven cost inflation as consumers increasingly choose alternative beverages.
