Agriculture outlook

Agriculture

US agriculture is caught between structural collapse and margin divergence. The cattle herd hit a 75-year low, reshaping beef supply and creating wild price swings, while corn prices have crashed to $4.15/bushel after record 2025 production, leaving most grain farmers with $180 per-acre losses. Vertical integration by major processors is accelerating, squeezing wholesalers and independent operators. Beekeeping is imploding—55.6% annual colony losses from varroa viruses—yet pollination fees spike 15% because California almonds have no alternative. Meanwhile, tariffs add $200+ million annually to nursery and floriculture supply chains, collapsing traditional wholesale margins across flower, nursery, and produce distribution.

Treat volatility, biology, and trade exposure as core economics rather than background noise.Prefer sectors with distribution leverage, controlled-environment advantages, or strong ties to processing and infrastructure rather than pure commodity exposure.
