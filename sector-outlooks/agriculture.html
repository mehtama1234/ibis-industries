Agriculture

Agriculture in 2025-2026 is being reorganized by cross-cutting pressures, with the economics increasingly determined by who can absorb complexity and sit on the right side of sector bottlenecks.

Treat volatility, biology, and trade exposure as core economics rather than background noise.Prefer sectors with distribution leverage, controlled-environment advantages, or strong ties to processing and infrastructure rather than pure commodity exposure.

Agricultural Insurance

The US agricultural insurance industry provides federally-backed crop protection to farmers, with government subsidies now reaching $10.4 billion annually and the market expanding as climate risks mount.

Dairy Farms in the US

In 2026, USDA forecasts higher U.S. milk production of 236.6 billion pounds but a lower all-milk price of $20.00 per cwt, pushing farm-level milk receipts down from an estimated 2025 peak even as cheese, butterfat and protein-oriented dairy products support demand.
