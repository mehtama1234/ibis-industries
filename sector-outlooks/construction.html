Construction

Construction in 2025-2026 is being reorganized by Space, Housing, and Local Friction, Work Without the Old Firm, Physical Reindustrialization and Infrastructure, with the economics increasingly determined by who can absorb complexity and sit on the right side of sector bottlenecks.

Follow bottlenecks: labor, permitting, power, and specialized scope matter more than generic backlog bragging.Own scarce trades, infrastructure-linked contractors, and disciplined operators positioned beside unavoidable build cycles.

Societal Read

Construction is being pushed by broader social and institutional strain through Space, Housing, and Local Friction, Work Without the Old Firm, where labor availability, coordination burden, and managed systems increasingly shape the market.

Space, Housing, and Local FrictionWork Without the Old Firm
Core tensions
  • The central tension inside Space, Housing, and Local Friction is that where Americans live, commute, and locate activity is being reorganized by housing lock-in, office impairment, land scarcity near infrastructure, and the uneven value of local place, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by Office Obsolescence Is a Structural Shift.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Adaptive Reuse Becomes a New Development Discipline.
  • The central tension inside Work Without the Old Firm is that the traditional firm is thinning. Expertise is rented more often, junior pathways are weaker, benefits are externalized, and automation is increasingly used to defend margins against labor scarcity, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by The Fractional Executive Layer Expands.
Signals
  • Adaptive reuse, housing lock-in, and power-adjacent land become recurring decision points.
  • Advisory retainers, project staffing, and portable admin layers gain relevance.
  • Automation, scheduling discipline, retention tactics, and selective simplification accelerate.
Operator implications
  • Office Obsolescence Is a Structural Shift implies that office exposure should be segmented by asset quality and adaptability, not averaged.
  • Adaptive Reuse Becomes a New Development Discipline implies that reuse work requires technical coordination and permitting fluency.
  • Housing Lock-In Distorts Mobility and Spend implies that transaction-light housing environments can still support strong maintenance and retrofit demand.
Investor implications
  • Capital should underwrite the operators that can routinize the new behavior, not those merely describing it in narrative terms.
