Where it shows up

Representative industries

Healthcare

Acupuncturists

A 7-9% growth industry offering needle-based pain and stress relief treatments, increasingly covered by insurance and integrated into mainstream healthcare as an alternative to pharmaceuticals.

Healthcare

Adoption & Child Welfare Services in the US

Adoption and child welfare providers are benefiting from steady federal and philanthropic funding, but shrinking foster-care caseloads, fewer adoptions from foster care and workforce shortages are shifting growth toward prevention, kinship care, counseling and post-permanency support.

Healthcare

Adult Day Care

The industry is a fragmented, Medicaid-heavy community-care market benefiting from population aging and the shift away from nursing homes, but margins are constrained by staffing costs, state reimbursement rates and tighter HCBS oversight.

Healthcare

Allergists

Allergists are consolidating from solo/small group practices into multispecialty networks, facing Medicare reimbursement cuts, at-home testing competition, and margin pressure while the overall allergy care market grows 8-9% annually.

Signals
