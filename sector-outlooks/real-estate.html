Apartment Rental in the US

Apartment rents have stopped climbing and flat-lined after years of double-digit growth, exposing oversupply in the Sun Belt while rent-controlled markets squeeze landlords between frozen pricing and soaring costs, driving industry consolidation and bankruptcies.

Real Estate

Commercial Real Estate in the US

The US commercial real estate sector is experiencing a marked but uneven recovery in 2025-2026, driven by industrial and multifamily strength while office properties face ongoing distress and adaptation pressures.

Real Estate

Conveyancing Services in the United States

Conveyancing services—property title and settlement work for residential and commercial real estate—is a 26 billion-dollar mature industry being reshaped by high mortgage rates crushing home sales, commercial M&A offsetting losses, and rapid automation adoption reducing closings delays.

Homeowners' Associations in the US

Homeowners' associations, condominium associations and housing cooperatives collect recurring assessments to govern shared property, fund maintenance, provide amenities and enforce community rules across a large share of US housing.

Societal Read

Real Estate is being pushed by broader social and institutional strain through Space, Housing, and Local Friction, Aging, Care, and the Assistance Economy, where labor availability, coordination burden, and managed systems increasingly shape the market.

Space, Housing, and Local FrictionAging, Care, and the Assistance Economy
Core tensions
  • The central tension inside Space, Housing, and Local Friction is that where Americans live, commute, and locate activity is being reorganized by housing lock-in, office impairment, land scarcity near infrastructure, and the uneven value of local place, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by Office Obsolescence Is a Structural Shift.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Adaptive Reuse Becomes a New Development Discipline.
  • The central tension inside Aging, Care, and the Assistance Economy is that an older America is creating durable demand across care, insurance, home services, devices, and finance, but labor shortages and payer control determine who actually captures that demand, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by Home-First Aging Reorders the Care Stack.
Signals
  • Adaptive reuse, housing lock-in, and power-adjacent land become recurring decision points.
  • Demand tilts toward chronic care, assistance, protection, and age-adapted convenience.
  • Margin performance diverges more from volume growth in payer-shaped sectors.
Operator implications
  • Office Obsolescence Is a Structural Shift implies that office exposure should be segmented by asset quality and adaptability, not averaged.
  • Adaptive Reuse Becomes a New Development Discipline implies that reuse work requires technical coordination and permitting fluency.
  • Housing Lock-In Distorts Mobility and Spend implies that transaction-light housing environments can still support strong maintenance and retrofit demand.
