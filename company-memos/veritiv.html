What's happening

The cleaning supplies distribution market is in structural decline from industry consolidation and direct-to-consumer competition, even as green product adoption and tariff volatility reshape competition and margins. Packaging and labeling services are growing as a resilience service layer for direct-to-consumer, healthcare, and regulated compliance workflows. Paper wholesaling remains in structural decline as digital substitution pressures volume, while packaging-grade demand and efficient fulfillment anchor profitability.

The investor question

Can Veritiv's distribution scale and customer relationships offset the structural decline in core cleaning supplies and pure paper distribution? Success depends on whether packaging and compliance-workflow services can grow fast enough to replace lost cleaning-supplies volume.

The operator playbook

Shift portfolio from commodity cleaning supplies toward higher-margin packaging and compliance services; invest in fulfillment and technical support to build customer stickiness. Lock in supply contracts and manage tariff exposure through strategic sourcing diversification.

The core tension

Local owner-operators and pure-play intermediaries cannot absorb margin compression or invest in service diversification; they will be acquired or forced out. If direct-to-consumer and online procurement accelerate faster than services can grow, distribution value evaporates.

Bottom line: Veritiv survives if services diversification and scale offset structural decline in core distribution; pure middlemen face acquisition or exit.
