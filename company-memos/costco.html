Costco

Contested Middle
What's happening

Warehouse club demand remains resilient as consumers seek value and product discovery in a bifurcating market. Tariff exposure is being managed through supply-chain optimization and selective pricing. E-commerce competition is mounting, but Costco's membership model and in-store experience create stickiness.

The investor question

Costco's moat is membership and procurement leverage. The question is whether membership and experience can remain defensible against Amazon and other e-commerce players.

The operator playbook

Costco must expand membership services beyond retail (financial, health, travel), optimize supply chain to manage tariff exposure, and maintain treasure-hunt assortment and low prices.

The core tension

Costco's scale gives it procurement advantage, but membership alone is not enough to defend against e-commerce convenience.

Bottom line: Costco wins if membership and experience stay more valuable than e-commerce convenience.
