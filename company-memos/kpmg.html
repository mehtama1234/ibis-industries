Advantaged
What's happening

Regulatory compliance is becoming a demand engine in itself—identity, fraud, and trust infrastructure are thickening. AI adoption is creating new advisory needs around workforce planning and system integration. M&A activity is supporting transaction services. Talent attrition and wage pressure are compressing margins in lower-tier service delivery.

The investor question

KPMG's advantage is that compliance and AI advisory create multi-year engagements. The risk is that clients hire in-house capabilities faster than KPMG can scale advisory depth.

The operator playbook

KPMG must shift capacity aggressively from transactional audit and tax to advisory, invest in AI and automation expertise, and retain senior partners through equity and brand positioning.

The core tension

KPMG is hired for compliance; it wants to own strategy. If clients treat KPMG as a temporary advisor rather than a trusted extension, margins compress.

Bottom line: KPMG must own the AI strategy conversation or become a commodity service provider.
