Advantaged
What's happening

OTC allergy and cold medicine demand rebounded in 2025 after weak 2024, but cold/flu incidence, retail inventory discipline, and FDA scrutiny limit upside. Oral rinse and vitamin markets grow on preventive-health trends. Tariff and raw-material volatility pressure margins.

The investor question

The question is whether Haleon's premium brand portfolio can maintain pricing power despite private-label competition and regulatory changes to OTC formulations. Consumer switching costs and retail shelf space are real moats, but commodity pressure is persistent.

The operator playbook

Defend brand premium through marketing and innovation while expanding into higher-margin segments like preventive oral health. Accept input-cost inflation and invest in supply-chain resilience.

The core tension

Brand switching costs erode as generic/private-label alternatives proliferate. Middle-market erosion squeezes mid-tier brands; value machines and premium refuges both gain share.

Bottom line: Haleon's brands are sticky, but raw-material costs and regulatory scrutiny erode margins faster than price increases can compensate.
