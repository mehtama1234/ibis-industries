Bayer

Contested Middle
What's happening

Generic pharmaceutical manufacturing is being crushed by PBM-driven price cuts and import competition, forcing a strategic pivot toward complex molecules and biosimilars. Crop-science consolidation is ongoing, with margins under pressure from input costs and farmer distress. Tariffs and chemical commodity volatility are compressing industrial chemical margins.

The investor question

Bayer's future depends on whether it can build scale and profitability in specialty pharma and biosimilars faster than generic and crop-science decline. Patent cliffs and regulatory tightening create execution risk.

The operator playbook

Bayer must accelerate exit from commodity chemicals and low-margin generics, invest aggressively in complex molecules and biosimilar manufacturing, and consolidate crop-science through precision agriculture.

The core tension

Bayer is reshaping itself from a commodities company to a specialty player. During the transition, legacy businesses are bleeding.

Bottom line: Bayer's transformation must complete before cash generation slows.
