What's happening

Medical supplies wholesaling remains growth-oriented on aging demographics and home-care expansion, but supplier margins compress from competitive bidding and compliance controls. Healthcare consolidation concentrates buyer power; online channels erode territorial control.

The investor question

The question is whether Henry Schein's distribution scale and healthcare regulatory moat withstand supplier pressure, margin compression, and direct-channel erosion. Consolidation creates scale but erodes pricing.

The operator playbook

Deepen customer relationships through value-added services and compliance automation while holding margin on consumables. Supplier consolidation and buyer aggregation both erode traditional distributor economics.

The core tension

Suppliers integrate forward (Amazon Logistics, retailer private networks); buyers consolidate (hospital systems, DSOs). Independent distributors face existential pressure; scale matters but doesn't guarantee profitability.

Bottom line: Henry Schein survives through distribution scale, but both ends of the supply chain are eroding its leverage.
