Unilever

Contested Middle
What's happening

Ice cream demand is shifting from bulk regular ice cream to premium and plant-based novelties. Mayonnaise is stable but under value pressure as ingredient costs rise. Salad dressing is bifurcating into premium refrigerated/functional variants and bargain-basement value. Across all categories, tariff exposure and ingredient volatility are compressing margins.

The investor question

Unilever's bet is that innovation in premium sub-segments and portfolio mix discipline can sustain margins even as volume in traditional formats declines. The risk is that premiumization efforts fail to offset mass-market volume loss.

The operator playbook

The operator must accelerate reformulation toward health-aligned products, ruthlessly cut SKUs in declining segments, and invest in premium positioning and e-commerce distribution. Cost-of-goods management is critical.

The core tension

Unilever's heritage is mass-market scale. But mass-market is fragmenting into premium and value. If the premium upside doesn't materialize, Unilever is trapped.

Bottom line: Unilever must own premiumization or die in the hollowed middle.
