What's happening

Small-business software adoption remains resilient as digitization and AI-assisted accounting normalize. Intuit's tax-prep and accounting software dominate but face regulatory and competitive pressure. Platform ecosystem expansion into lending and payments creates new revenue streams and stickiness.

The investor question

The core question is whether Intuit's tax-prep dominance and small-business customer lock-in are defensible or whether AI automation and regulatory change (simplified tax filing) compress margins. Customer lifetime value and ecosystem upsell matter more than tax-return volume.

The operator playbook

Expand platform ecosystem beyond accounting into lending, payments, and business services. Invest in AI automation to reduce complexity and support higher-touch advisory services.

The core tension

Regulatory scrutiny threatens tax-prep monopoly; AI automation threatens pricing. Workflow lock-in is real, but customers increasingly expect integrated experiences.

Bottom line: Intuit's tax-prep moat is under pressure, but platform ecosystem and customer relationships offer long-term defensibility.
