Advantaged
What's happening

PTFE coatings and paint manufacturers are balancing moderate domestic demand in architectural and industrial markets against higher compliance and input-cost volatility in 2025-2026. Sustainable building materials are benefiting from higher construction activity and stricter performance standards, even as certification timelines reshape profitability. Automotive coatings remain tied to automaker production volumes, which are volatile.

The investor question

Does PPG's installed relationships with builders, manufacturers, and refinish shops give it pricing power and margin durability against tariff and input-cost shocks? The answer depends on whether volume and mix can grow or hold steady through volatility.

The operator playbook

Lock in PTFE and specialty coating supply contracts and build proprietary formulations where tariff exposure is lower; invest in sustainable building materials because regulation and construction demand are supporting growth. Manage automotive exposure by diversifying customer base.

The core tension

Import-reliant assemblers and subscale producers cannot weather tariff shocks or sustain capex investment; they will be consolidated. If input costs spike faster than pricing can adjust or if construction demand softens, profitability deteriorates.

Bottom line: PPG wins if scale and specialty positioning let it maintain margins through volatility; smaller competitors face acquisition or margin compression.
