Industry base

The big picture is not just that the economy is changing. It is that daily life, business structure, and institutional behavior are changing together: households are splitting into value and premium, health is becoming a social norm engine, care is aging into a labor bottleneck, AI is turning into both a power trade and a workflow trade, and scale owners keep taking more of the spread created by complexity. This version adds a denser read inside each theme, with explicit drivers, pressure points, signals, and strategic consequences at the subtheme level.

How To Read It

What This Layer Adds

The force map says what keeps repeating across industries. This themes layer says what those repetitions mean for American life: how people spend, how institutions ration, how firms hire, how place changes, and where power is concentrating.

Recurring Questions

  • Is this category a value machine, a premium refuge, or dead-center middle?
  • Does this category align with health, fight it, or need to be reformulated around it?
  • Is this product really competing with other products, or with experiences?
  • Where is demand guaranteed but return capture uncertain?
  • What work must stay internal and what can be rented or automated?
  • Where are political supply chains changing cost structure permanently?
  • Is this market still hospitable to independents, or has the structure already shifted?
  • Is this demand discretionary, or is it effectively mandatory?
  • Which physical places are getting more valuable and which are losing strategic purpose?
  • Where is AI substituting labor and where is it creating new physical bottlenecks?
