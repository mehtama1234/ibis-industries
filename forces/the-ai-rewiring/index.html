A force from the data · Technological · 2025–2026

The AI Rewiring

AI automates the routine cognitive work — and the value moves to whoever deploys it, owns the data, or sells the judgment a machine can't.
How we found this This isn't a hunch — it's what fell out of reading 543 US industries side by side. The same force kept reappearing. Each page below is one corner of the economy where it shows up, with the real 2025–2026 numbers as proof.
The one idea

Artificial intelligence made routine cognitive work cheap and gave permanent advantage to whoever owns the algorithm, controls the data, or operates at scale.

Creative & knowledge work gets automated

Design & the web

Video & content

Words & publishing

The back office thins out

Accounting & audit
The machines are taking the grunt work, the liability is staying human, and the junior accountant has nowhere left to hide.
Insurance claims & broking
AI triages claims and prices risk faster than humans; the middle layer of adjusters and brokers thins out as the work moves in-house or consolidates upward.
Law & consulting
AI does the draft; the pyramid cracks.
C

Even diagnosis & advice

Medical diagnostics
AI reads scans and labs faster and cheaper than humans; volume rises, per-read price falls, and the physician's gate remains the only moat.
Money & advice
AI makes financial advice routine. Winners own scale, data, and the judgment machines can't yet replicate.
Education & credentials
<em>AI tutors are free and good. What a college degree actually sells now is not what it used to.</em>

The new scarcities

The deploy dividend
The wins go to whoever sits between the AI and the enterprise—not the ones being disrupted.
The human premium
AI is making taste, judgment, and accountability scarce — and whoever can prove a person stands behind the work is winning.
The junior-role collapse
AI eats the training work, and nobody has answered who becomes the senior of tomorrow.

The big picture

The AI rewiring
Routine cognitive work got cheap; judgment, data, and control got scarce and valuable.
