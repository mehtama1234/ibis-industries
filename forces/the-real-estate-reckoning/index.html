The Real-Estate Reckoning

Hybrid work broke the office and higher rates broke the math — capital rotates from offices to logistics, data centers, and housing, and a debt wall forces the reckoning.
How we found this This isn't a hunch — it's what fell out of reading 543 US industries side by side. The same force kept reappearing. Each page below is one corner of the economy where it shows up, with the real 2025–2026 numbers as proof.
The one idea

Hybrid work and higher rates broke the old real-estate leverage game, forcing capital to rotate from offices toward logistics warehouses, data centers, and housing—and a $2.8-trillion debt wall through 2028 is forcing the reckoning.
