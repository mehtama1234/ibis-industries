A force from the data · Technological · 2025–2026

The Electrification

The car goes electric and software-defined — and the whole century-old machine of engines, transmissions, gas, and exhaust starts dying while batteries, chips, and copper take over.
How we found this This isn't a hunch — it's what fell out of reading 521 US industries side by side. The same force kept reappearing. Each page below is one corner of the economy where it shows up, with the real 2025–2026 numbers as proof.
The one idea

The electrification made batteries expensive to build and copper expensive to find, while engines and gas stations became structurally unprofitable, squeezing suppliers and operators caught in between the old machine and the new one.
