How we found this This isn't a hunch — it's what fell out of reading 543 US industries side by side. The same force kept reappearing. Each page below is one corner of the economy where it shows up, with the real 2025–2026 numbers as proof.
The one idea

Across dentistry, senior care, home improvement, and commercial real estate, independent practitioners and business owners are being absorbed into scale platforms because debt loads, reimbursement pressure, and labor scarcity make going alone unsustainable.
