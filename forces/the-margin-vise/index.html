A force from the data · Economic · 2025–2026

The Margin Vise

Labor, materials, energy, and tariffs rise faster than pricing power — margins compress, and only the automated, the premium, or the locked-in escape the squeeze.
How we found this This isn't a hunch — it's what fell out of reading 543 US industries side by side. The same force kept reappearing. Each page below is one corner of the economy where it shows up, with the real 2025–2026 numbers as proof.
The one idea

When costs rise faster than prices, only the consolidated, automated, and premium survive—and everyone else becomes redundant.
