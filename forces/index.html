Forces from the Data

We read 1491 US industries side by side and asked one question: what forces keep showing up? These are the 20 that do — each built out into its own collection, with the real numbers as proof.
The method

This is the companion to the Strategy Under a Force series — but grounded in hard 2025–2026 industry data instead of headlines. Every claim traces back to a specific industry we researched. 187 write-ups across 20 forces and five lenses.
