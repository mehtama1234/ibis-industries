Elderly & Disabled Services as a Care Demand Platform

A demographically advantaged care category where staffing, reimbursement, and service coordination cap who can actually convert demand into returns.

Representative industry

Elderly & Disabled Services in the US

Elderly and disabled services providers deliver nonmedical home aide, adult day, companionship, transportation and community support services for older adults and people with disabilities, with demand increasingly tied to Medicaid HCBS policy and the preference to age in place.

Why this case matters

Business interpretation

This is a service operator business that wins when aging households, working parents, and health complexity create durable demand for care businesses, but labor and regulation cap returns.

Demographic demand is durable, but staffing, regulation, and service coordination favor scaled operators with strong labor systems.

Child Education & Developmental Center Franchises

The US child education and developmental center franchise industry has recovered from the 2020 pandemic shock and is around a 2025 market size of about $3.9-$4.0 billion, but growth is modest because tuition hikes, wage pressure and declining births offset strong demand for reliable, curriculum-based care.

2025: about $3.9-$4.0 billion for US child education and developmental center franchises, estimated from the 2022 baseline of $3.68 billion and the 2022-2028 projected CAGR of 1.67%, consistent with public franchise-market references around $4.0 billion.2022-2025: about 1.7%-2.8% annualized depending on whether the 2022 baseline projection or rounded 2025 public market estimate is used; 2022-2028 baseline projection was 1.67% CAGR.2022: 14.5% EBIT margin in the baseline; 2025-2026 margins remain pressured by wages, insurance, rent and compliance costs despite tuition increases.

Family Counseling & Crisis Intervention Services in the US

Family counseling and crisis intervention providers deliver nonresidential counseling, support groups, crisis hotlines, family welfare, substance-use support, shelter referrals and related social assistance, with 2025-2026 momentum tied to 988 capacity, CCBHC expansion, block-grant funding and persistent behavioral-health need.

2026: $52.4 billion estimated industry revenue in 2021 dollars from the 2022 IBISWorld forecast; broader Census NAICS 6241 Individual and Family Services revenue reached $194.2 billion in 2025 and $49.3 billion in Q1 2026 seasonally adjusted.2021-2026: 1.8% forecast CAGR for the IBIS industry; broader Census NAICS 6241 revenue grew 7.5% in 2025 versus 2024 and Q1 2026 seasonally adjusted revenue was 6.5% above Q1 2025.2021: 3.5% EBIT margin in the IBIS baseline, reflecting nonprofit reinvestment and labor intensity.

Family Planning & Abortion Clinics in the US

US family planning and abortion clinics generated an estimated $3.9 billion in 2025 revenue, with demand supported by contraceptive care, STI testing, Title X and Medicaid reimbursement, while abortion access has shifted sharply toward protected states and telehealth.

2025 estimated revenue: about $3.9 billion, derived from the 2022 IBIS baseline of $3.73 billion and its 1.8% 2022-2027 forecast CAGR; latest public Census/FRED NAICS 621410 employer revenue totaled $3.891 billion in 2022 across taxable and tax-exempt firms.2022-2027 forecast CAGR: 1.8%; 2022 revenue growth: 1.1%; 2017-2022 CAGR: 2.0%.2022 estimated EBIT margin: 7.8%; 2027 baseline forecast: 7.7%.

In-Home Senior Care Franchises
