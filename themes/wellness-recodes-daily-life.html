Evidence industries
  • Weight Loss Services in the US Consumer Services
    The US weight loss services industry has shifted from center-based coaching and branded diet plans toward hybrid behavioral, clinical and subscription models as GLP-1 medications pull demand away from legacy programs while expanding the addressable market for medically supervised weight management.
  • Fast Food Restaurants in the US Food & Drink
    A mature, fragmented $424B industry growing below 1% in 2026 as traffic deflates from consumer retrenchment and GLP-1 adoption, labor costs rise structurally (California $20/hour minimum now core cost floor), tariffs push food prices relentlessly higher, and chains make desperate bets on AI automation to replace payroll.
  • Candy Production in the US Food & Drink
    The US candy industry is shifting from high-sugar commodity production toward premiumized, functional, and texture-driven innovations, while facing unprecedented tariff-driven cost inflation and structural competition from chocolate and imported goods.
  • Cookie, Cracker & Pasta Production in the US Manufacturing
    A stable, maturing industry pivoting to health-conscious products faces margin squeeze from tariffs, labor costs, and commodity inflation while major players consolidate to compete across premium and value tiers.

Functional Consumption Beats Empty Indulgence

Shoppers increasingly want food and drink to do something specific: energize, hydrate, recover, sharpen focus, or support long-term health.

energy and hydration as daily performance categoriessupplement-like branding migrating into mainstream beveragesprotein and gut-health language broadening beyond athleteswellness framing becoming a permission structure for premium pricing
The Health ReckoningThe Hollow Middle
Evidence industries
  • Energy Drink Production Manufacturing
    The industry manufactures and markets caffeinated ready-to-drink beverages, shots and mixes, with 2025 demand supported by strong retail velocity, flavor innovation, expanded distribution and consumer substitution away from soda and some coffee occasions.
  • Juice Production in the US Food & Drink
    A maturing $13.6 billion industry facing structural demand collapse—consumers no longer believe juice is healthy—while hit by tariffs, regulatory pressure on sugar, and Florida's decimated citrus harvest.
  • Health Stores in the US Retail
    The brick-and-mortar health stores sector is stalled—market growth is slowing, major chains are in bankruptcy or being sold to private equity, and tariffs on vitamins and supplements are forcing difficult cost-pass-through decisions in 2025-2026.
  • Coffee & Snack Shops in the US Consumer Services
    Pricing power is gone: commodity shocks and tariffs have crushed margins, bankruptcies hit independents, mega-deals consolidate the top (Keurig/JDE Peet's), while Starbucks staggers through a labor-heavy turnaround and QSRs poach coffee sales via convenience and automation.
