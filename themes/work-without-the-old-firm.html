Evidence industries
  • Business Certification & IT Schools in the US Technology & Digital
    The US business certification and IT schools industry is bifurcating: high-margin enterprise and compliance-linked credentials (cloud, cybersecurity) are growing at 12-16% annually, while commodity bootcamps and general IT courses face bankruptcies and commoditization as AI and free learning platforms make basic skills training structurally uncompetitive.
  • Educational Services in the US Business Services
    Educational services in the US—spanning K-12, higher ed, and training—remains the largest in the sector, but enrollment is fleeing public schools for private/charter alternatives, while AI and online platforms squeeze margins and force consolidation among smaller players.
  • Electricians in the US Business Services
    The US electricians industry is being pulled between a dead residential market and explosive demand from AI/EV/grid infrastructure, creating labor shortages, wage pressure, and consolidation among the 269,000 small contractors.
  • Heating & Air-Conditioning Contractors in the US Construction
    The US HVAC industry is a $159 billion mature market (2026) being reshaped by residential weakness, commercial surge, regulatory refrigerant shifts, heat pump electrification, and a severe technician labor shortage that is pushing wages up 8% annually.
