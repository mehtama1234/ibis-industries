# Company / Business Lens Template

Use this template when writing up an individual company, local operator, platform, franchise, or business model on top of the industry corpus.

The point is not to produce a generic profile. The point is to anchor the business inside the repo's force map.

## 4. What Kind of Demand Is This?

Classify the demand system:

- `demand_type`:
  - cyclical
  - demographic
  - policy-driven
  - reimbursement-driven
  - consumer preference-driven
  - convenience-driven
  - premium/status-driven
  - mission-critical / non-discretionary

Questions:

- Does demand exist because people want it, need it, are reimbursed for it, or are required to buy it?
- Is growth volume-driven, mix-driven, or pricing-driven?
- Is the demand wave structural or temporary?

## 7. What Operator Type Wins Here?

- `best_owner_type`:

  - scaled regional platform
  - PE roll-up
  - franchise operator
  - software-led consolidator

Questions:

- Who is naturally advantaged in this business?
- Does the business reward hustle, systems, capital, brand, or technical depth?
- Is this a good independent business, a roll-up candidate, or a scale game?

## 8. What Should The Reader Believe?

Finish with a concise point of view:

> `company_or_business` is best understood as a `_____` business exposed to `_____` forces. The upside comes from `_____`; the real risk is `_____`; the advantaged operator is `_____`.

### Likely Winners

### Likely Losers

## Minimal Output Structure

When converting this template into a final written page, keep the structure tight:

1. one-paragraph business truth
2. industry and force context
3. demand system
4. binding constraint
5. economics and margin logic
6. operator/owner implication
7. bottom-line judgment
