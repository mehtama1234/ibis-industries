What to do

The operating question inside energy and environmental infrastructure is which constraint actually governs the spread pool: demand capture, staffing, compliance, procurement, capital intensity, utilization, reimbursement, or infrastructure access.

What to underwrite

The investor question is which version of energy and environmental infrastructure owns the bottleneck, routinizes the complexity, and keeps the new behavior legible enough to hold pricing, throughput, or renewal power.

Tensions

This is an infrastructure operator that wins when regulated assets, field-service density, and mission-critical environmental or energy systems create durable demand.
