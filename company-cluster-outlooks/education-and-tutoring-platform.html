Evidence industries
  • Online Tutoring Services Technology & Digital
    In 2025–2026, demand from schools and families, stronger AI integration, and platform consolidation are reshaping online tutoring toward higher volume, lower friction, and more software-led differentiation.
  • Tutoring & Driving Schools in the US Consumer Services
    Tutoring and driving schools sell supplemental academic help, exam preparation, driver education and other non-degree instruction to households, schools and institutions, with 2025-2026 demand supported by high-dosage tutoring adoption, digital SAT participation and steady teen-driver safety needs but pressured by online price competition and funding delays.
  • After-School Program Providers Consumer Services
    After-school program providers remain a fragmented, low-margin care and enrichment industry with strong 2025 family demand, constrained access, staffing pressure and heavy exposure to public funding decisions.
  • Tutoring & Test Preparation Franchises Consumer Services
    Tutoring & Test Preparation Franchises sell recurring K-12 academic support, math and reading programs, and admissions-test preparation through branded local centers and hybrid franchise models, with 2025-2026 demand supported by learning-loss concerns but constrained by digital competitors and uneven test-prep demand.
