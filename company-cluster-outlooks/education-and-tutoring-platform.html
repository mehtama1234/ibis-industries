Bottom line: Outcomes credibility and distribution scale both matter, but neither alone is enough.

The core tension

Distribution and technology can be scaled, but building credible outcomes and customer acquisition channels takes years and won't yield venture returns.

Who's advantaged
  • Networks with proven track records of measurable student improvement and strong brand trust
  • Hybrid models that combine local presence or franchise partnerships with digital tools
Who's exposed
  • Point-solution tutoring platforms struggling to prove outcomes against established competitors
  • Purely digital players without offline distribution or measurable credibility in local markets
Tensions
