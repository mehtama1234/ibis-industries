Evidence industries
  • Mayonnaise Production Food & Drink
    US mayonnaise production remains largely stable through 2025-26, with category resilience supported by branded innovation even as value pressure and ingredient volatility persist.
  • Seasoning, Sauce and Condiment Production in the US Manufacturing
    The category is still growing in 2026, but growth is increasingly concentrated in higher-value and differentiated products while producers absorb higher input and labor costs and defend share against private label.
  • Table Salt Production Manufacturing
    USGS-linked 2026 figures show about 40 million tons of domestic production and about USD 2.6 billion in U.S. salt sold/used (2025), while FDA/CDC data show average sodium intake remains above recommendations, keeping demand composition and formulation strategy under structural pressure.
  • Chocolate Production in the US Food & Drink
    A $28.9 billion mature market fragmenting into price-compressed mass brands and premium e-commerce winners, pressured by cocoa tariffs and shifting consumer health priorities.
  • Industrial Chocolate Production Manufacturing
    Industrial Chocolate Production is a concentrated, commodity-driven manufacturing industry whose 2025 dollar revenue has been lifted by record cocoa prices even as customer volumes and processing margins have been pressured.
  • Cocoa & Drinking Chocolate Production Manufacturing
    The industry primarily makes powdered hot cocoa and drinking chocolate mixes for retail, foodservice, wholesalers, and specialty channels, with 2025-2026 performance driven more by cocoa input costs and price realization than by strong volume growth.
