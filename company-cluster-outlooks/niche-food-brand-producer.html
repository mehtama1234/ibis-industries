Bottom line: Health-driven demand is real but fragile; retailers hold the actual pricing power.

The core tension

Consumer preference changes create new demand windows, but procurement volatility and retailer power destroy profitability before scale arrives.

Who's advantaged
  • Multi-channel producers (retail, foodservice, direct) that reduce retailer dependence
  • Brands with genuine consumer loyalty that justify shelf space during margin compression
Who's exposed
  • Single-channel producers locked into retailer relationships with declining bargaining power
  • Trend-dependent brands that lose shelf space when health fads shift or input costs spike
Tensions

Niche food makers navigate health-driven demand, input volatility, and retailer leverage through differentiation.
