    The US commercial real estate sector is experiencing a marked but uneven recovery in 2025-2026, driven by industrial and multifamily strength while office properties face ongoing distress and adaptation pressures.
  • US Commercial Leasing 2025–2026: The Tale of Two Markets Real Estate
    The US commercial leasing industry is splitting into thriving segments (prime office, industrial, AI datacenters) and structural decline (secondary office, traditional retail) as hybrid work, shifting capital, and e-commerce permanently reshape real estate demand.
  • Real Estate Appraisal in the US Real Estate
    The industry is still in mild decline, but technology, digital submission rules, and AMC-led distribution are reshaping margins and workflows.
  • Public Storage & Warehousing in the US Transport & Logistics
    Third- and fourth-party operators are gaining share in high-touch fulfillment and healthcare/data-center storage niches, but margin growth is being constrained by wages, automation spend, and competitive pricing.
  • Real Estate Investment Trusts in the US Real Estate
    In 2026, US REITs expanded to about $1.474T in all-REIT equity market capitalization, improved operating returns, and kept financing risk contained while investors rotate toward sectors with stronger demand and AI-related tailwinds.
  • Mobile Storage Services Consumer Services
