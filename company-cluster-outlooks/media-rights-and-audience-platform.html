Evidence industries
  • Cable Networks in the US Media & Entertainment
    Cord-cutting has pushed cable TV from serving 88% of US households in 2010 to just 42% by 2026, forcing industry giants to divest cable networks and concentrate on streaming while advertising revenue falls 6-8% annually.
  • Magazine & Periodical Publishing in the US Media & Entertainment
    US magazine publishing is contracting in print while pivoting to digital subscriptions and premium positioning, facing simultaneous headwinds from tariffs, AI content disruption, and collapsing print advertising.
  • Amusement Parks in the US Consumer Services
    The industry is enjoying strong nominal growth driven by pent-up leisure demand and blockbuster new attractions, but real per-guest margins are being eroded by sticky labor costs, tariff-driven equipment expenses, and mounting pressure from consumers increasingly pricing-conscious about family vacations.
  • Newspaper Publishing in the US Media & Entertainment
    US newspapers face a structural decline from digital disruption, print advertising collapse, and consolidation, with only subscription-focused national outlets and nonprofit models showing viability.
  • US Business Information Resellers Industry Business Services
    A mature, consolidating reseller business that aggregates market data and research for subscription—now competing with free AI, tighter privacy rules, and customer fatigue.
  • Movie & Video Distribution in the US Media & Entertainment
    The sector is shrinking in traditional distribution but stabilizing overall as streaming-dominant windowing and hybrid licensing models pull more demand into recurring subscription video.
