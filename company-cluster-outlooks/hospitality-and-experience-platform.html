Bottom line: Dynamic pricing, brand loyalty, and flexible labor beat asset accumulation.

The core tension

Lowering asset ownership was supposed to reduce risk, but it also reduces pricing power; you're left optimizing margins on marginal occupancy, not returns on invested capital.

Who's advantaged
  • Brands with loyalty programs and data-driven pricing that smooth demand seasonality
  • Platforms with flexible unit economics and ability to adjust staffing to occupancy
Who's exposed
  • Asset-heavy operators with fixed labor costs and low flexibility in downturns
  • Undifferentiated or mid-market brands caught between luxury and budget segments
Tensions

This is an experience platform that wins when brand, occupancy, distribution, and asset-light operating systems convert travel and leisure demand into repeatable unit economics.
