Bottom line: Narrow specification moat only holds if you're the only maker or customers accept no alternatives.

The core tension

Specification defensibility is real but fragile; raising labor or capital without raising prices forces margin compression.

Who's advantaged
  • Manufacturers of critical components for large OEMs where requalification is prohibitively expensive
  • Producers of custom-engineered products that lock customers through legacy systems
Who's exposed
  • Commodity industrial producers competing on price with larger, capital-rich rivals
  • Specialized manufacturers whose moat erodes as competitors enter or in-house alternatives emerge
Tensions

Narrow, specified products that defy substitution defend small manufacturers against larger competitors.
