The investor question is which version of utilization-managed experience venue owns the bottleneck, routinizes the complexity, and keeps the new behavior legible enough to hold pricing, throughput, or renewal power.

This is a service operator business that wins when experience businesses survive the channel shift when the venue itself is the product and utilization can be managed tightly.
