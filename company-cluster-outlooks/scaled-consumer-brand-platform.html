  • RTD Coffee Production in the US Manufacturing
    US RTD coffee producers in 2025–26 are gaining from strong convenience demand and premium wellness variants, but category share is pressured by competition and high input costs.
  • US Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting Industry 2025-2026 Agriculture
    The US agriculture sector faces a perfect storm—historic drought, record labor costs, and tariff dislocations—that has collapsed margins for most crops while paradoxically tightening beef supplies to historic lows, creating divergent fortunes across animal and crop subsectors.
  • US Corn Farming Industry Agriculture
    Record 2025 corn production at 17.0 billion bushels crashed prices to $4.15/bushel, leaving farmers facing $180-per-acre losses in 2026 while tariffs, consolidation, and rising input costs reshape a 196,000-farm industry that has contracted at 4.7% annually over five years.
  • US Farm Supplies Wholesaling Agriculture
    A mature, consolidating industry built on narrow margins, now under severe pressure from farm income collapse and digital disruption of its traditional distribution model.
