Evidence industries
  • Beauty, Cosmetics & Fragrance Stores in the US Retail
    US beauty retail is stable at $69 billion (2026) but structurally hollowed by e-commerce and social commerce, with specialty players holding margin through premiumization while smaller brands fail and consumer tariff-driven retrenchment looms.
  • Perfume & Fragrance Stores Retail
    Perfume and fragrance specialty retail grew only 0.9% in 2025 after earlier stronger rebounds, with online beauty channels lifting category demand while in-store malls keep losing share.
  • Online Perfume & Cosmetic Sales Retail
    The U.S. Online Perfume & Cosmetic Sales industry sells skincare, makeup and fragrance through websites, apps, marketplaces and omnichannel retailer platforms, with 2026 revenue estimated at $17.8 billion after a 2025 beauty-market rebound and continued growth in U.S. ecommerce penetration.
  • Personal Trainers Consumer Services
    As of 2025–2026, personal training remains a high-frequency discretionary wellness service with growing engagement intensity in-person and online, but pressure on margins and session depth is pushing trainers toward specialization and bundled coaching models.
  • Gym & Fitness Franchises Consumer Services
    The US gym and fitness franchise industry operates branded franchised fitness centers and studios that earn recurring membership, class, training, and ancillary revenue while relying on franchisor systems for site selection, marketing, technology, equipment purchasing, and operating standards.
  • Gym, Health & Fitness Clubs in the US Consumer Services
    The industry sells recurring access to fitness facilities, classes and training, with 2025 demand at record highs but margins still shaped by rent, labor, equipment reinvestment and intense local price competition.
