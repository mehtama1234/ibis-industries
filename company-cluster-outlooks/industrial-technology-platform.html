  • Ice Cream Production in the US Food & Drink
    The US ice cream market is growing slowly (2.7% annually to 2031) as big manufacturers restructure via M&A, tariff pressures mount, and consumer spending shifts from bulk regular ice cream to premium, single-serve, and plant-based novelties.
  • Mayonnaise Production Food & Drink
    US mayonnaise production remains largely stable through 2025-26, with category resilience supported by branded innovation even as value pressure and ingredient volatility persist.
  • Meal Replacement Product Manufacturing Manufacturing
    The industry remains in modest structural growth through 2026, with premium protein, fortified, and RTD formats expanding faster than traditional formats.
  • Synthetic Fiber Manufacturing in the US Manufacturing
    The industry in 2026 is likely to grow slowly overall, with value creation concentrated in technical fibers, recycled-content offerings, and downstream applications that can absorb input-cost volatility.
  • Wood Pulp Mills in the US Manufacturing
    The industry remains mature and volatile, with 2025 demand supported by tissue, fluff pulp and packaging uses but pressured by weaker paper grades, softer export prices and capacity rationalization.
  • Shoe & Footwear Manufacturing in the US Manufacturing
    US shoe and footwear manufacturing generated about $1.8 billion in 2025, down 0.2% year over year, as domestic factories supplied only a tiny share of a footwear market still dominated by Asian imports.
