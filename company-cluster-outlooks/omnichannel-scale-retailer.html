Bottom line: Fulfillment control and supplier leverage drive omnichannel profits, not channel presence alone.

The core tension

Fulfillment capital is massive and locks in cost structures for years; but outsourcing means you're hostage to someone else's network, making the capex choice existential.

Who's advantaged
  • Integrated retailers with unified inventory and owned logistics networks
  • Platforms with sufficient scale to dictate terms to suppliers and replace underperforming fulfillment
Who's exposed
  • Multi-channel retailers with fragmented inventory and outsourced logistics
  • Smaller pure-plays that can't achieve the scale required for supplier negotiation
Tensions

This is a scaled merchant-platform business that wins when traffic, fulfillment, supplier terms, and customer data are centralized across physical and digital channels.
