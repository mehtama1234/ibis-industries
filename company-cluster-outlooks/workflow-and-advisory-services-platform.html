Bottom line: Embed so deep that client switching costs exceed the cost of keeping you.

The core tension

Talent scarcity makes scaling harder than deploying software, while clients expect AI-driven productivity gains without paying more for human expertise.

Who's advantaged
  • Platform incumbents with decade-old installations—switching is organizational suicide
  • Operators who train client staff to depend on their tools and know-how
Who's exposed
  • Small practices that sell high-touch expertise without repeatable workflows
  • Generalists competing against AI-assisted automation in commodity advisory tasks
Tensions

This is a workflow and advisory services platform that wins when expertise, process discipline, and recurring client workflow embed the operator inside a business or institutional system.
