Capital requirements and utilization risk are real, but winners will own regional niches with sticky customer bases.

Bottom line: You survive if you're faster to resupply or cheaper to finance than a marketplace.

The core tension

Platform competition and supplier-direct models erode margin, yet distributors still need capital to stock inventory and extend credit.

Who's advantaged
  • Distributors with dense regional networks and established credit relationships
  • Operators who bundle technical support and inventory localization into their value
Who's exposed
  • Distributors with high capital intensity and low utilization in slowdown periods
  • Middle-tier players without brand loyalty or technical differentiation losing share to aggregators
Tensions

This is a distributor business that wins when distribution businesses are attractive when inventory, credit, technical knowledge, or local availability makes the middleman hard to remove.
