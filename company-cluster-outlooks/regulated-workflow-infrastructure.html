What to do

The operating question inside regulated workflow infrastructure is which constraint actually governs the spread pool: demand capture, staffing, compliance, procurement, capital intensity, utilization, reimbursement, or infrastructure access.

What to underwrite

The investor question is which version of regulated workflow infrastructure owns the bottleneck, routinizes the complexity, and keeps the new behavior legible enough to hold pricing, throughput, or renewal power.

Tensions

This is a claims/admin/compliance layer business that wins when complexity creates businesses: testing, claims, identity, forensic work, hr, and compliance software turn regulation into recurring demand.
