The Memos

Societal memo

Societal Systems Memo

Thesis: The biggest societal pressure is not raw need. It is the system burden created by aging, housing friction, and thinner institutional support.

Operator angle: Reduce coordination load for households or institutions. That is where the durable pain sits.

Investor angle: Prefer rails around care, housing immobility, benefits fragmentation, and managed workflows over labor-heavy exposure without system leverage.

Aging, Care, and the Assistance EconomyWork Without the Old FirmSpace, Housing, and Local Friction
Best hunting grounds
  • family-coordination and reimbursement infrastructure
  • portable admin, payroll, and benefits support for fragmented work
  • housing-lock-in beneficiaries tied to local maintenance, rental support, and neighborhood services
  • businesses turning institutional friction into repeatable workflow
Watch for
  • rising coordination burden hidden inside categories that still look demand-rich
  • sectors where labor shortage and administrative drag cap monetization
  • new dependence on households to manage more of the system themselves
Core tensions
  • The central tension inside Aging, Care, and the Assistance Economy is that an older America is creating durable demand across care, insurance, home services, devices, and finance, but labor shortages and payer control determine who actually captures that demand, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by Home-First Aging Reorders the Care Stack.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Senior Housing Demand Meets Staffing and Capacity Friction.
  • The central tension inside Work Without the Old Firm is that the traditional firm is thinning. Expertise is rented more often, junior pathways are weaker, benefits are externalized, and automation is increasingly used to defend margins against labor scarcity, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by The Fractional Executive Layer Expands.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Skills and Credentials Replace Slow Career Ladders.
Signals
  • Demand tilts toward chronic care, assistance, protection, and age-adapted convenience.
  • Margin performance diverges more from volume growth in payer-shaped sectors.
  • Advisory retainers, project staffing, and portable admin layers gain relevance.
  • Automation, scheduling discipline, retention tactics, and selective simplification accelerate.

Cultural memo

Cultural Reclassification Memo

Thesis: Culture is changing what Americans consider healthy, respectable, social, and identity-bearing, and that is reorganizing category economics.

Operator angle: Design for the new permission structure around health, affiliation, participation, and self-presentation.

Investor angle: Prefer businesses that benefit when category legitimacy shifts toward wellness, community, moderation, and experience.

Wellness Recodes Daily LifeExperience, Status, and CommunityWork Without the Old Firm
Best hunting grounds
  • wellness-coded consumption and recurring self-improvement routines
  • experience formats with repeatable cultural or community gravity
  • affiliation and fandom ecosystems with durable monetization loops
  • service models that help customers feel competent, modern, or socially legible
Watch for
  • old cultural scripts still embedded in product design or store logic
  • vice and indulgence categories that have lost social permission faster than they have lost revenue
  • categories where status migrated from ownership to participation
Core tensions
  • The central tension inside Wellness Recodes Daily Life is that health behavior is no longer a niche vertical. It is becoming a general operating system for food, drink, beauty, self-presentation, and everyday social decisions, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by GLP-1 Resets Appetite and Portion Economics.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Sober Socializing Changes Nightlife and Beverage Mix.
  • The central tension inside Experience, Status, and Community is that more consumer status is being expressed through participation, memory, aesthetics, and curation rather than through ownership of broad mid-tier goods, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by Memory Beats Merchandise.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Venue Scarcity Creates Durable Pricing Power.
Signals
  • Protein, low-sugar, sober-curious, and health-tracking language becomes more central.
  • Mix shifts toward either obvious value packs or visibly better premium tiers.
  • Adaptive reuse, housing lock-in, and power-adjacent land become recurring decision points.
  • Advisory retainers, project staffing, and portable admin layers gain relevance.

Consumer memo

Consumer Selection Memo

Thesis: The consumer is still spending, but the bar for what deserves the spend is much harsher and much more explicit.

Operator angle: Be legible fast: prudent, premium, convenient, healthy, or meaningful. The muddled middle gets punished.

Investor angle: Own value systems, premium refuges, convenience rails, and identity-rich categories rather than generic mid-tier exposure.

Barbelled Consumer AmericaWellness Recodes Daily LifeExperience, Status, and Community
Best hunting grounds
  • scale value formats with strong own-brand economics
  • premium niches with visible quality justification
  • convenience businesses that remove real friction rather than just add fees
  • retail and service formats that combine identity, habit, and experience
Watch for
  • trade-down without shame in categories once protected by brand status
  • categories where convenience survives even as broad pricing power weakens
  • mid-market formats still assuming broad undifferentiated demand will come back
Core tensions
  • The central tension inside Barbelled Consumer America is that the old middle of American consumption keeps losing coherence. Households polarize between ruthless value-seeking and selective premium spend, while undifferentiated mid-market operators get squeezed from both sides, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by Value Machines Win the Weekly Basket.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Premium Refuges Still Hold Price.
  • The central tension inside Wellness Recodes Daily Life is that health behavior is no longer a niche vertical. It is becoming a general operating system for food, drink, beauty, self-presentation, and everyday social decisions, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by GLP-1 Resets Appetite and Portion Economics.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Sober Socializing Changes Nightlife and Beverage Mix.
Signals
  • Mix shifts toward either obvious value packs or visibly better premium tiers.
  • Traffic, basket construction, and repeat behavior move toward low-friction channels.
  • Protein, low-sugar, sober-curious, and health-tracking language becomes more central.
  • Adaptive reuse, housing lock-in, and power-adjacent land become recurring decision points.

Industrial memo

Industrial Control Memo

Thesis: The industrial winner is increasingly the business that controls the stack, the bottleneck, or the mandatory workflow rather than just the visible product.

Operator angle: Follow the real choke points: power, land, labor, compliance, procurement, and system ownership.

Investor angle: Prefer infrastructure, regulated workflow, specified capability, and scale-control positions over generalized growth stories.

Physical Reindustrialization and InfrastructureMachine Intelligence and Compute BuildoutRegulated Software and the Admin StateScale, Financialization, and the Owned Economy
Best hunting grounds
  • power- and utility-linked infrastructure
  • compliance and trust rails embedded in required workflows
  • specified manufacturing and buildout bottlenecks
  • asset or governance layers that capture the spread created by complexity
Watch for
  • AI or reshoring narratives detached from real physical constraints
  • operators serving growth without owning any scarce layer of the stack
  • middlemen that can be compressed by scale owners or workflow software
Core tensions
  • The central tension inside Physical Reindustrialization and Infrastructure is that the physical economy has become strategic again. Tariffs, reshoring, power demand, logistics, and infrastructure needs are repricing materials, locations, and the trades that make modern buildout possible, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by Tariffed Inputs Flow Through the Whole Stack.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Power-Centric Geography Reorders Industrial Value.
  • The central tension inside Machine Intelligence and Compute Buildout is that aI is not just a software feature cycle. It is simultaneously a workflow-compression story, a compute and power infrastructure story, and a market concentration story around who can afford the stack, but the route to capturing that demand runs through the practical frictions surfaced by The Data-Center Land Rush Creates New Scarcity.
  • A second tension sits between household or institutional demand and the operating constraints surfaced by Workflow Compression Rewrites Knowledge Work.
Signals
  • Domestic capability, logistics resilience, and buildout bottlenecks matter more in valuation.
  • Automation budgets, inference demand, and power-ready site competition keep rising.
  • Workflow software, certification, and managed compliance budgets keep deepening.
  • Verification, underwriting precision, and workflow adjacency become stronger moats.
