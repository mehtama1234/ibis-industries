The US gym and fitness franchise industry operates branded franchised fitness centers and studios that earn recurring membership, class, training, and ancillary revenue while relying on franchisor systems for site selection, marketing, technology, equipment purchasing, and operating standards.

HVAC Service Franchises

The US HVAC Service Franchises industry is a small but resilient home-services franchise niche, supported by recurring repair demand, aging building systems, energy-efficiency upgrades and national franchisors that help local operators with branding, dispatch, software, training and purchasing power.

2025: about $1.2 billion in US franchise outlet revenue; 2026: estimated $1.23 billion-$1.28 billion based on baseline 2025 level and low-single-digit 2026 demand indicators. 2020-2025: +4.8% CAGR in the baseline forecast; 2026: low-single-digit estimated revenue growth as repair/replacement offsets weaker new housing starts. 2025: 5.6% EBIT margin in the baseline forecast, up from 4.8% in 2020.
