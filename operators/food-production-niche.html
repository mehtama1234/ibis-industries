The Food Production Niche

Manufacturing

Cocoa & Drinking Chocolate Production

The industry primarily makes powdered hot cocoa and drinking chocolate mixes for retail, foodservice, wholesalers, and specialty channels, with 2025-2026 performance driven more by cocoa input costs and price realization than by strong volume growth.

About $1.1 billion in 2026 US producer revenue, estimated on a nominal basis from the 2021 IBISWorld baseline and 2024-2026 cocoa-cost pass-through. About 3% to 5% nominal revenue growth in 2026, with unit demand softer than dollar sales because 2025-2026 pricing is carrying much of the increase. About 12% to 16% EBIT margin in 2026, down from the 2021 baseline margin of 19.3% because cocoa, sugar, dairy, packaging, and tariff costs absorbed part of price realization.

Dairy Wholesaling in the US

US dairy wholesalers remain essential to cold-chain distribution, but 2025-2026 growth is restrained by retailer and processor bypass, soft domestic fluid-milk demand, volatile milk prices and rising logistics costs, while export strength in cheese and butterfat supports upstream volume.

Egg & Poultry Wholesaling in the US

The industry is a mature, low-margin food distribution niche where steady poultry demand is offset by volatile egg pricing, avian influenza disruptions, and rising competition from broadline distributors and vertically integrated processors.

2026 estimate: $12.9 billion in revenue, based on the 2022 IBISWorld $12.3 billion baseline and its 2022-2027 forecast to $13.1 billion. 2022-2027 baseline forecast: 1.2% CAGR; 2026 conditions are mixed, with USDA forecasting 2026 broiler prices up 1.6% and 2026 egg prices down 30.4% after the 2025 HPAI-driven spike. 2022 baseline: 2.2% EBIT margin; 2027 baseline forecast: 2.2% EBIT margin.
