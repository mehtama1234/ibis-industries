Coal & Ore Wholesaling in the US

The US Coal & Ore Wholesaling industry remains a mature, low-concentration distribution market whose 2025 revenue base is still near $19 billion, but 2026 demand indicators point lower as utility coal consumption retreats while nonfuel mineral demand stays supported by metals, critical-mineral and industrial supply-chain needs.

2025 estimated revenue: about $18.9 billion, using the 2021 IBISWorld baseline revenue of $19.2 billion and its 2021-2026 projection of roughly $18.8 billion, adjusted for the 2025 EIA coal-consumption rebound. 2025 estimated revenue growth: about +0.5% year over year; 2026 demand signal: negative, with EIA forecasting US coal consumption down 8.8% from 452 million short tons in 2025 to 412 million short tons in 2026. 2021 baseline profit margin: 2.7%; 2026 margin pressure remains high because commodity resale spreads are narrow and logistics, inventory and credit risks are material.

Egg & Poultry Wholesaling in the US

The industry is a mature, low-margin food distribution niche where steady poultry demand is offset by volatile egg pricing, avian influenza disruptions, and rising competition from broadline distributors and vertically integrated processors.

2026 estimate: $12.9 billion in revenue, based on the 2022 IBISWorld $12.3 billion baseline and its 2022-2027 forecast to $13.1 billion. 2022-2027 baseline forecast: 1.2% CAGR; 2026 conditions are mixed, with USDA forecasting 2026 broiler prices up 1.6% and 2026 egg prices down 30.4% after the 2025 HPAI-driven spike. 2022 baseline: 2.2% EBIT margin; 2027 baseline forecast: 2.2% EBIT margin.

Industrial Machinery & Equipment Wholesaling in the US

Industrial machinery and equipment wholesalers make money by buying specialized industrial equipment from manufacturers and reselling it to manufacturers, oil and gas operators, warehouses, machine shops, contractors and other industrial users, with demand tied closely to capital spending, industrial production, oil prices and warehouse automation.

2025 estimated revenue: $330.2 billion, indexed from the 2022 IBIS revenue baseline to BLS/FRED NAICS 4238 sectoral output. 2025 estimated revenue growth: 2.4% versus 2024, using BLS/FRED NAICS 4238 sectoral output growth from $650.3 billion in 2024 to $665.9 billion in 2025. 2022 IBIS profit margin: 4.1%; 2025 margins remain pressured by wage, freight, inventory-carrying and tariff-sensitive metals costs.
