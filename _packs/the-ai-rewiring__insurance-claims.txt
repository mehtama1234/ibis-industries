### Insurance Brokers & Agencies in the US (Finance & Insurance)
- Market size: $216-220 billion USD (2025) | growth: 0.6% to 2% annually (2024-2025); historical average 2.6% CAGR | margin: 14-17% net margin at industry level (2025-2026); smaller independents operate at 2-10%, large diversified firms capture majority of margin gains
- Takeaway: In 2025-2026, the $216B market is growing modestly (0.6-2% annually) but reeling from a 70,000-job employment cliff, mega-acquisitions concentrating power among five giants, and commission compression in health and workers compensation lines.
- Current dynamics: The industry is bifurcating. Large brokers are absorbing mid-market rivals at accelerating speed (854 announced transactions in 2025, the third-highest on record), consolidating power and technology capabilities. Small independents face existential pressure: 30,000 of them generate under $1.25M annually with no clear internal succession. Simultaneously, labor is leaving the industry faster than new entrants arrive. The Bureau of Labor Statistics projects nearly 400,000 retirements by end-2026, and actual payroll data shows the insurance sector shed 70,000 jobs in the 12 months through June 2026 despite nominal GDP growth. AI is no longer hype: agentic AI is the most-pursued technology (projected to grow from $5.76 billion in 2025 to $7.26 billion in 2026), with appetite matching, document extraction, and submission intake delivering measurable ROI.
