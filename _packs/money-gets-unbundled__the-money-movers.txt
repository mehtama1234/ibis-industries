### Custody, Asset & Securities Services in the US (Finance & Insurance)
- Market size: $46.1 billion (2026) | growth: 3.7% CAGR 2021-2026; 1.9% growth in 2026 | margin: 26.9% (2026) | businesses: Approximately 30,975 establishments (2026) | employees: 76,500+ employees (2026)
- 2022 baseline: $32.952 billion (2021)
- Takeaway: The custody, asset and securities services industry is expanding at 3.7 percent annually on the back of digitization and alternative asset growth, but faces intense fee pressure, concentration among four dominant banks, and an urgent pivot to blockchain and cryptocurrency custody to capture the next wave of wealth management.
- Current dynamics: The industry is undergoing a fundamental transformation driven by three forces. First, digital assets are exploding: crypto custody (projected at $834 billion globally in 2026) attracts both traditional custodians (BNY Mellon, State Street, Fidelity) and specialized fintech players (Fireblocks, Anchorage, BitGo), with the OCC permitting national banks to offer crypto custody since March 2025 and approving five new digital-asset bank charters in December 2025. Second, fee compression is relentless—global custody fees are under intense pressure, squeezing margins even as assets under administration grow, forcing firms to automate ruthlessly and diversify into higher-margin services like risk analytics and compliance AI. Third, regulatory modernization is forcing compliance overhauls: the SEC's Custody Rule Modernization (NPRM planned for April 2026) will require explicit provisions for crypto assets and updated onboarding, identity verification, and recordkeeping for digital instruments, demanding significant IT and process investments.
