- Takeaway: US background checks face a ceiling: regulatory complexity now matters more than innovation, hiring cycles determine demand, and global expansion is where real growth lies.
- Current dynamics: Hiring cycles—not market expansion—now drive revenue. Companies that cut hiring pause background checks; those that ramp up demand them. This volatility is the industry's defining trait in 2025-2026: tech layoffs in early 2025 slowed demand, but hiring began thawing mid-year. Regulatory burden has exploded. Ban-the-box laws now cover nearly 40 states and 150+ cities; Texas joined in September 2025, and Washington State and Philadelphia tightened rules in 2026. The Fair Chance to Compete for Jobs Act, cannabis screening bans, and a 30%+ jump in FCRA litigation year-over-year through 2025 have forced providers to build compliance expertise, not just speed. AI is becoming table-stakes. Checkr and others now use ML to flag inconsistencies and verify identities in real time, but the CFPB's October 2024 circular raised questions about whether AI-driven adjudication itself falls under FCRA, creating legal risk.
