- Current dynamics: The industry is experiencing a perfect storm of margin compression. PBMs have become the true price-setters: they control 80% of the market and are now facing federal and state regulatory scrutiny after the FTC documented massive markups on specialty generics and filed antitrust suits against Caremark, Express Scripts, and OptumRx. In February 2026, Congress enacted legislation to delink PBM compensation from drug prices and move to flat service fees—this is the most significant regulatory intervention in decades, but implementation won't ease pricing pressure for manufacturers. Meanwhile, imports from India and China now account for 58% of US pharmaceutical imports by weight, and India ships $10.5 billion in pharma to the US annually while depending on China for 70% of its raw materials. This global supply chain is under political scrutiny: the Trump administration has signaled 100% tariffs on generic drugs by August 2028, and the PILLS Act would incentivize US-based manufacturing.
