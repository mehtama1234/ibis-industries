### Newspaper Publishing in the US (Media & Entertainment)
- Market size: $29.6 billion (2026) | growth: -3.2% annualized (2021-2026); declined 4.7% in 2026 alone | margin: Tight and declining; advertising-dependent business model under pressure
- Takeaway: US newspapers face a structural decline from digital disruption, print advertising collapse, and consolidation, with only subscription-focused national outlets and nonprofit models showing viability.
- Current dynamics: Print advertising revenue crashed from $73.2 billion in 2000 to $6 billion in 2023 to $407.7 million in 2024, a near-total collapse in what was historically the industry's lifeblood. Digital advertising, expected to replace print, has proved disappointing at just $315.1 million in 2024 and projected only $5.5 billion by 2028—far below print's former levels. Circulation revenue has stabilized slightly as major metros (New York Times, Bloomberg, Wall Street Journal) successfully monetize paywalls; NYT added 250,000 digital subscribers in Q1 2025 alone. But this success is limited to 10-15 national and large regional outlets with brand value; thousands of small-town and mid-size papers have no paywall strategy that works.
