- Takeaway: HMO providers are a mature, consolidating sector where Medicare Advantage growth masks steep profitability declines, employer-market retreat, and emerging regulatory pressure on cost justification and network adequacy.
- Current dynamics: Medical loss ratios (MLRs) are the core crisis: 17% of MA contracts ran above 100% MLR in 2023, and another third sit in the 90–95% range with no margin cushion. Prescription drugs, especially GLP-1s like Ozempic, are driving surprise costs.
