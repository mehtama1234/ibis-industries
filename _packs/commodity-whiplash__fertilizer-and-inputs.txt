- Takeaway: Tariff chaos and commodity price collapse are destroying margins while technology and mega-merger consolidation push the industry toward a narrower, more automated power structure.
- Current dynamics: The industry faces a perfect storm: commodity prices (corn $4.20/bu, soybeans $10.30/bu, wheat $5.00/bu projected for 2026) sit 15-37% below farmer breakeven costs, crushing downstream wholesale margins. Tariff escalation peaked in April 2025 (125% reciprocal tariffs) before partial easing in November 2025, but the damage lingers—China shifted buying to South America, and US soybean exports fell 13% year-over-year in 2025/26.
