### Beauty, Cosmetics & Fragrance Stores in the US (Retail)
- Market size: $69.3 billion (2026) | growth: 2.5% to 3.3% annually (2020–2026) | margin: High (10%+ for top players like Ulta); pressured by tariffs and online competition | businesses: 172,000 establishments (2025) | employees: 454,095 (2025)
- 2022 baseline: $29.3 billion (2022, specialty-only definition)
- Takeaway: US beauty retail is stable at $69 billion (2026) but structurally hollowed by e-commerce and social commerce, with specialty players holding margin through premiumization while smaller brands fail and consumer tariff-driven retrenchment looms.
- Current dynamics: The industry is bifurcating. Ulta Beauty posted $12.4 billion in net sales for fiscal 2025 (ended Feb 2026), up 9.7% YoY, with comp sales up 5.4%, driven by higher average ticket and new store sales; it's guiding to 6–7% growth in fiscal 2026. Meanwhile, specialty beauty startups and smaller multi-brand retailers are failing fast: Glossier closed 75% of stores, Pat McGrath Labs filed Chapter 11 in early 2026, and Flower Beauty shut in September 2025. The biggest structural shift is that Amazon and TikTok Shop (which posted $2.49 billion in US beauty GMV in 2025 alone with 123% YoY growth) are cannibalizing discovery and repeat purchases. By 2030, Amazon's beauty share is forecast to climb to 15% from 10% in 2024, while Sephora and Ulta's combined share dips from 20% to 19%.
