- Takeaway: The US business certification and IT schools industry is bifurcating: high-margin enterprise and compliance-linked credentials (cloud, cybersecurity) are growing at 12-16% annually, while commodity bootcamps and general IT courses face bankruptcies and commoditization as AI and free learning platforms make basic skills training structurally uncompetitive.
- Current dynamics: The industry is splitting into two tiers. CompTIA Security+, AWS certifications, and Azure AI Engineer credentials now command premium pricing because employers link them directly to hiring and insurance discounts.
