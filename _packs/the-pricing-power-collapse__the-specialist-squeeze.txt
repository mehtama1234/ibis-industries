- Takeaway: The US OB-GYN industry is a $17.2 billion (2026) healthcare specialty caught between rising demand and a shrinking workforce, with abortion restrictions pushing doctors out of conservative states and forcing consolidation into large health systems.
- Current dynamics: Abortion restrictions after Dobbs are reshaping geographic supply. Residency applications to restricted states declined 6.7% in 2024 while applications to abortion-legal states rose 0.4%. Simultaneously, birth rates are at historic lows—3.6 million births in 2025 (down 1% from 2024), a 23% decline since 2007. This paradoxically boosts demand for contraceptive and preventive care (which OB-GYNs provide) even as obstetric/maternity caseloads shrink.
