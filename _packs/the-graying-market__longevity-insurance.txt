- Takeaway: A mature, well-capitalized $1.1 trillion industry growing at 0.6% in 2025 but contracting 1.4% in 2026 due to interest rate normalization, though annuity sales hit record $464 billion in 2025 on the back of four million annual retirees and rising consumer demand for guaranteed income.
- Current dynamics: The industry is experiencing a paradox: record-high annuity sales (464.1 billion in 2025, up 7%) are masking slower underlying growth and a generational crisis in life insurance adoption. Fixed-indexed annuities nearly doubled to $126 billion in 2024, and the newer RILA product class (Registered Index-Linked Annuities) surged 270% from $24 billion in 2020 to $65 billion in 2024, driven by affluent retirees seeking guaranteed income in a volatile market. But individual life insurance sales are decelerating—LIMRA projects just 1% growth in 2026 after flat 2025—because Gen Z and younger millennials overestimate term life costs by 10-12x and lack traditional triggers (marriage, children) that drove prior generations' purchases. Meanwhile, the Fed's interest rate easing (cutting from highs) is dampening fixed annuity demand; rising private credit allocations (31.4% of portfolios by end 2024) reflect intensifying search for yield.
