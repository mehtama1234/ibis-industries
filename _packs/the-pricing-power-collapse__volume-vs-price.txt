- Takeaway: The US home care industry is growing due to an aging population preferring care at home, but faces existential margin compression from stacked payment cuts, severe workforce shortages at 75% annual turnover, and regulatory complexity that is driving consolidation into larger platforms.
- Current dynamics: Medicare payment cuts hit home care providers for four straight years, with 2026 seeing a 1.3% aggregate reduction ($220 million) after a 6.4% cut was initially proposed.
