Real Estate

Operator angle: Read assets through present-day flows of work, housing, logistics, and power rather than old category labels.

Investor angle: Prefer logistics, utility-linked land, adaptive reuse capability, and assets aligned with current demand geometry over weak commodity office exposure.

Advantaged setups
  • Businesses positioned on the right side of Space, Housing, and Local Friction.
  • Operators with real system leverage: scale, workflow, procurement, or distribution control.
  • Formats with visible differentiation rather than generic middle positioning.
Exposed setups
  • Undifferentiated middle-market operators without pricing power.
  • Businesses carrying rising complexity without enough scale or workflow leverage.
  • Models exposed to secular behavior change but still priced as if old demand patterns hold.
  • Asset- or labor-heavy operators exposed to input volatility without pass-through rights.
Diligence questions
  • Which of the dominant themes in Real Estate are structural rather than cyclical?
  • Where does margin actually get lost: labor, procurement, compliance, capital, or channel access?
  • Does the operator own a bottleneck, or is it merely exposed to one?
  • Which physical constraint matters most here: labor, land, power, materials, utilization, or permitting?
