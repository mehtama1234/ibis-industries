Advantaged setups
  • Businesses positioned on the right side of Regulated Software and the Admin State.
  • Operators with real system leverage: scale, workflow, procurement, or distribution control.
  • Formats with visible differentiation rather than generic middle positioning.
  • Embedded workflow and trust infrastructure with recurring necessity.
Exposed setups
  • Undifferentiated middle-market operators without pricing power.
  • Businesses carrying rising complexity without enough scale or workflow leverage.
  • Models exposed to secular behavior change but still priced as if old demand patterns hold.
  • Middlemen and service layers that can be compressed by software or platform control.
Diligence questions

  • Where does margin actually get lost: labor, procurement, compliance, capital, or channel access?
  • Does the operator own a bottleneck, or is it merely exposed to one?
  • How much of the economics are governed by reimbursement, regulation, fraud control, or trust infrastructure?

AD&D Insurance

The US AD&D insurance market is a mature, consolidating industry where falling interest rates, automation, tightening reinsurance regulation, and tariff-driven inflation are eroding margins faster than modest volume growth can restore them.

Agricultural Banks

Automobile Insurance

The US auto insurance industry swung from unsustainable losses (2023-2024) into a narrow profit window (95.3 combined ratio in 2025) as rate hikes moderate and AI-powered telematics reshape pricing, but margin compression looms in 2026 as competition returns and repair-cost inflation persists.

Business Insurance

The US business insurance industry is a mature, consolidated market worth $288.3 billion (2025) that posted its strongest underwriting results in a decade, but faces deepening pressure from climate-driven losses, litigation-driven claim inflation, and AI-driven disintermediation threatening the 87% share held by traditional independent agents.
