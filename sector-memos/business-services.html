Work Without the Old Firm

The Fractional Executive Layer Expands

Leadership bought by project or part-time retainer and specialist expertise replacing generalist middle management are no longer isolated category quirks. They are showing up across business services, where the fractional worker and the margin vise are changing the operating baseline. That pushes the category toward a world where advisory businesses should package expertise into recurring, productized offers. The pattern is already visible in example operators such as Accenture, WPP plc, and ADP.

The Fractional WorkerThe Margin Vise
Pressure points
  • Business services operators have to absorb this shift in a market where business coaching in 2025-2026 is shifting from a discretionary C-suite perk to a scaled, AI-augmented professional development tool for mid-market and small-business leaders, underpinned by measured ROI claims and driven by a fragmented but rapidly professionalizing workforce of 165,000+ coaches.
  • Business services operators have to absorb this shift in a market where the US consulting industry is growing but hollowing: AI is replacing junior work, federal spending is collapsing, and clients are demanding outcomes not hours.
Signals and consequences
  • Advisory retainers, project staffing, and portable admin layers gain relevance.
  • Operators lean harder on pricing architecture, pack-size changes, and cost takeout.
  • Advisory businesses should package expertise into recurring, productized offers.
  • Clients increasingly buy outcomes, not hours alone.
