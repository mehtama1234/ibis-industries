          Education and Tutoring Platform

          Learning-support platforms consolidate fragmented tutoring demand through curriculum credibility, distribution, and measurable outcomes. The channel shift accelerates digital delivery and reduces dependency on physical location. Scale helps spread curriculum development and marketing cost, but customer acquisition remains expensive because educational trust is earned slowly. The compute super-cycle enables personalized learning, yet real outcomes still depend on human attention. Winners balance technology scale with local partnerships or franchise models.
